MANGALORE REFINERY AND PETROCHEMICALS LIMITED
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP) MANUAL
SECTION 14: ASSET INTEGRITY MANAGEMENT AND PRESSURE PIPING CODE COMPLIANCE

14.1 SCOPE AND COMPLIANCE STANDARDS
All hydrocarbon carrying pressure piping within the refinery battery limits must adhere strictly to ASME B31.3 (Process Piping Code) and OISD-STD-129 (Inspection of Pressure Vessels).

14.2 CORROSION ALLOWANCE AND WALL THICKNESS THRESHOLDS
- For crude distillation furnaces and high-temperature transfer lines, a minimum nominal corrosion allowance of 3.0 mm must be maintained over the theoretical pressure-design thickness.
- If ultrasonic thickness testing indicates a measured wall thickness (t_actual) within 0.5 mm of the code-mandated minimum thickness (t_min), the equipment must be flagged for mandatory turnaround retubing within 12 calendar months.

14.3 DELEGATION OF POWER (DOP) APPROVAL WORKFLOW
- Routine engineering integrity approvals for equipment operating with > 3.0 years of calculated remaining life fall under the approving authority of the Chief General Manager (Technical Services).
- Any emergency shutdown, derating of operating pressure, or capital replacement exceeding INR 50,00,000/- requires approval from the Executive Director (Refinery Operations).
